# Oracleo Fintech LLC – Enterprise GRC Programme Implementation

**Engagement Type:** Full-scope Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Programme Design  
**Industry:** Financial Technology (FinTech) – Digital Banking, Payments, Lending  
**Regions:** India, Singapore, United Kingdom  
**Frameworks:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022, NIST CSF 2.0, COBIT 2019, CIS Controls v8  
**GRC Platform:** ServiceNow IRM (Integrated Risk Management) – https://dev269444.service-now.com  
**Status:** Ready for Internal Audit & Regulatory Review

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This repository contains the complete, internally-consistent GRC implementation for **Oracleo Fintech LLC**, a 500-employee FinTech operating across three jurisdictions. The project harmonises information security, enterprise risk, compliance, vendor risk, and audit management into a unified framework.

All documents are cross-referenced, fully mapped to regulatory obligations (PCI DSS, SOC2, GDPR, India DPDP Act, RBI Guidelines), and structured exactly as delivered to a Fortune 500 financial institution.

**ServiceNow Integration:** Every module is configured and operational in ServiceNow IRM. Reference links to the live instance are embedded throughout.
